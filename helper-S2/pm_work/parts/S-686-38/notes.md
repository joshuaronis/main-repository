## Study facts (for the card and C074)
- Species: pigs (28 pigs, 56 median nerves exposed, one per brachial plexus; 6 excluded before randomisation, 3 for surgical injury to extrinsic vessels, 7 for probe dislocation; 40 nerves analysed, n = 10 per group). PDF p4.
- Nerve: median nerve, surgically exposed in the axilla (axillary brachial plexus) with extrinsic epineural vessels preserved. PDF p2.
- Application: test solution applied topically onto the surgically exposed nerve next to the probe ("applied directly to the median nerve next to the probe (resulting in spread under the probe due to capillary effects)"; "applied topically to the nerve"). Not an injection: not intraneural, not needle perineural. Extraneural/topical on the exposed epineurium. PDF p2, p3.
- Volume: a single fixed volume of test solution per nerve, the same in every group (PDF p2, p3) — the figure is stated only in the card and C074's reason.
- Epinephrine concentration: [dose figure omitted] in bupivacaine (BupiEpi) (PDF p3) — HIGHER (more concentrated) than the concentration named in C074; not the same. The figure is stated only in the card and C074's reason.
- Bupivacaine and clonidine concentrations: [dose figure omitted] (not asked; kept out).
- Groups: saline control; bupivacaine alone; bupivacaine + epinephrine; bupivacaine + clonidine. No epinephrine-alone arm, no clonidine-alone arm, no dexamethasone, no lidocaine.
- Measured: nerve blood flow by laser-Doppler flowmetry (relative to baseline, AU); tissue oxygen saturation SO2 (%) by tissue spectrophotometry (O2C device; capillary/post-capillary microvasculature, ~1–2 mm deep inside the nerve); relative tissue haemoglobin rHb (AU, venous congestion); systemic HR, MAP, SpO2. PDF p2–3.
- Period: 60 min after application (T0, T5, T10, T15, T20, T25, T30, T40, T50, T60); 10 min baseline settling before. PDF p2–3.
- Results by direction (Tables 2–5, PDF p5–7):
  - Bupivacaine alone: nerve blood flow fell progressively (median relative flow 0.70 at T5 → 0.50 at T30 → 0.33 at T60), significantly below saline at T30 and T60 (p < 0.0001); SO2 fell late (92 at T0 → 76 at T60), significantly below saline at T60 (p = 0.0005); rHb no difference.
  - Bupi + epinephrine: rapid early fall in flow (0.59 at T5, 0.45 at T10, 0.38 at T30, 0.41 at T60), not significantly different from bupivacaine alone (T30 p = 0.48; T60 p = 0.39); SO2 dipped early (median 74 at T10, 83 at T20) and recovered (91 at T60), not significantly different from bupivacaine; two nerves had severe desaturation (SO2 nadirs 3% at T20 and 10% at T10). Versus saline: flow lower at T30/T60, SO2 not different at T60 (p = 0.481).
  - Bupi + clonidine: flow fell like bupivacaine alone (0.45 at T30, 0.43 at T60), not significantly different from bupivacaine (T30 p = 1.0; T60 p = 0.19); SO2 84.5 at T60, not different from bupivacaine or saline.
  - Saline: flow ~1.0–1.2 to T50, 0.86 at T60; SO2 stable ~88–92.
  - Internal inconsistency: Results (PDF p5) report SO2 bupivacaine vs saline significant at T60 (p = 0.0005), Table 5 p = 0.003; the Discussion's first paragraph (PDF p5) says "no significant difference for tissue oximetry between bupivacaine versus saline". The abstract and Table 3 support the significant T60 difference.
- Funding: departmental funding. COI: TW consultancy fees from B. Braun; HW speaker fees Syntetica, B. Braun, Teleflex, Vygon; TS speaker fees B. Braun, Teleflex, Vygon (B. Braun makes the bupivacaine product used). PDF p8.
